# Phase 9: Local daily reminders

Phase 9 adds an optional daily study notification on Android.

## Behavior

- The user explicitly enables the reminder and chooses a time.
- Android asks for notification permission when required.
- The alarm is inexact to respect battery-saving behavior.
- Turning the reminder off cancels the scheduled alarm.
- The saved reminder is restored when the app starts.
- Unsupported platforms show a clear message and keep the control disabled.

All settings remain on the device. No account or network service is involved.
